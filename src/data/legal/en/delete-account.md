Deletion is final and you can do it yourself, inside the app. Here is how.

<h2 id="in-app">From the app</h2>

1. **Open your profile.** From the bottom bar, tap *Profile*.
2. **Go to the account settings.** [In-app path to be confirmed, e.g. Settings → Account]
3. **Tap "Delete account" and confirm.** We ask you to confirm. After that, there is no going back.

Already uninstalled the app or unable to sign in? Use the manual request below.

<h2 id="cosa">What gets deleted</h2>

- The account and sign-in data (Sign in with Apple or Google)
- Profile, level, XP, collectibles and predictions
- Group memberships and preferences
- [Any exceptions required by law, to be confirmed]

Timing: data is removed within [n] days of the request.

<h2 id="manuale">Manual request by email</h2>

Write to [support@rosanero.app](mailto:support@rosanero.app?subject=Account%20deletion) with the subject **Account deletion** from the email address linked to the account. We will confirm once the deletion is done.
