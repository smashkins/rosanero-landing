Rosanero is an **unofficial** app for Palermo F.C. supporters. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Palermo F.C. or any league or federation. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the app, why, and what rights you have.

The [Italian version](/privacy/) of this policy prevails in case of any discrepancy.

<h2 id="sez-1">1. Data controller</h2>

The data controller is **Vincenzo Stira**, who can be contacted at [support@rosanero.app](mailto:support@rosanero.app) for any request concerning your data.

No Data Protection Officer has been appointed, as the conditions requiring one do not apply.

<h2 id="sez-2">2. You can use Rosanero without an account</h2>

Fixtures, results and the squad are browsable **without signing up**. If you do not create an account we process no identifying data: only the technical and diagnostic data described in § 3.3, and the technical information needed to deliver content to you (§ 3.4).

<h2 id="sez-3">3. What we process</h2>

### 3.1 Account data

If you create an account, we process:

- your **name** and **email address**, and whether the email has been verified;
- your **profile picture**, if the provider you sign in with supplies one;
- your **password**, if you choose email and password sign-in — never stored in the clear, only as a cryptographic hash;
- the **6-digit verification codes** (OTP) sent by email, kept only for as long as they are valid;
- the **identifiers of your linked identities** (Apple, Google, email/password) and the tokens the provider issues for them.

If you use **Sign in with Apple** and choose "Hide My Email", we receive an anonymous `@privaterelay.appleid.com` forwarding address, not your real one.

### 3.2 Session data

To keep you signed in we process a **session token** and its expiry and — recorded by the server when the session opens — the device's **IP address** and **user agent**. The token is stored on your device in the system **Keychain**; the app also holds a short-lived token, kept in memory only, to reach protected content.

### 3.3 Diagnostic and usage data

Distributed builds (TestFlight and App Store) include Google's **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**, which collect aggregated usage events and crash reports with the device's technical state at the time of the error (model, OS version, app version, identifiers generated by the libraries). We do not use this data for advertising profiling and do not combine it with your account data. Internal development builds do not configure Firebase and send nothing.

### 3.4 Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, what you write is transmitted to the **FeedbackThread** service together with an identifier for your account, so that we can reply. Do not put data in that channel that you do not want processed.

### 3.5 Signing up for the beta on the website

If you sign up for the public beta on the website's [Public beta](/en/beta/) page, we process the **email address** you enter, the **platform** you pick (iPhone or Android), your **phone model**, the page language and the time of sign-up. We need the model to check that the app can be installed and to test it on a range of devices. This data reaches us by email at support@rosanero.app, through **Resend**, and we only use it to invite you to the test and to write to you during the beta: never for newsletters or ads. To invite you we add the address to **TestFlight** (Apple) or to the **Google Play** tester list, which send you the invite. To prevent abuse the server also uses the **IP address** of the request, only for as long as it takes to limit repeated submissions.

<h2 id="sez-5">5. Where the data comes from</h2>

From you directly, or — when you choose to sign in with Apple or Google — **from the provider**, which gives us an identifier, the associated email address and, where available, your name. We never receive your password for those services.

<h2 id="sez-6">6. Who we share it with</h2>

We do not sell your data and do not share it for third-party marketing. The following providers process it on our behalf, as processors or as independent controllers:

- **Apple** and **Google**, as identity providers, for sign-in;
- **Resend**, for sending verification and password-recovery emails and for forwarding beta sign-ups to us;
- **Apple (TestFlight)** and **Google (Google Play)**, when we invite you to the beta;
- **Google (Firebase)**, for diagnostics and usage analytics;
- **FeedbackThread**, for handling feedback;
- the **hosting provider** of the infrastructure the service runs on.

We may also disclose data to judicial or other competent authorities where the law requires it.

<h2 id="sez-7">7. Transfers outside the European Economic Area</h2>

Some of the providers listed above are based in the United States. Where this involves a transfer of data outside the EEA, it takes place on the basis of the European Commission's **standard contractual clauses** or an **adequacy decision**, as provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR.

<h2 id="sez-8">8. How long we keep it</h2>

- **Account data**: for as long as the account exists. If you delete it, the data attached to it is deleted with it (see § 9).
- **Sessions**: until they expire or you sign out.
- **Verification codes**: minutes — as long as they are valid.
- **Diagnostic data**: per Firebase's retention periods, and in any case no longer than the purpose requires.
- **Feedback**: for as long as it takes to handle the conversation with you.
- **Beta sign-ups**: until the end of the test programme, or earlier if you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete them.

<h2 id="sez-9">9. Deleting your account</h2>

You can delete your account **from inside the app**, under *More → Delete Account*. Deletion removes your profile, your linked identities and your sessions, and revokes the authorisation you granted to Apple if you had signed in with Apple. It is **irreversible**. Step-by-step instructions are on the [Account deletion](/en/delete-account/) page.

<h2 id="sez-10">10. Your rights</h2>

Under GDPR arts. 15–22 you have the right to **access** your data, to have it **rectified** or **erased**, to **restrict** processing, to **data portability**, and to **object** to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to [support@rosanero.app](mailto:support@rosanero.app) to exercise them; we will reply within one month. If you signed up for the beta you can also **withdraw your consent** at any time, at the same address: withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

You also have the right to lodge a **complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority** (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

<h2 id="sez-11">11. Children</h2>

The app is not intended for children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to [support@rosanero.app](mailto:support@rosanero.app) and we will delete it.

<h2 id="sez-12">12. Changes to this policy</h2>

We may update this policy; the date at the top always identifies the current version. If changes are substantial we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
