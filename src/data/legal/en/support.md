Rosanero is made by fans: we answer ourselves, not a call centre. Write to us for bug reports, questions about your data or account requests.

<h2 id="scrivici">Write to us</h2>

For anything, bugs, ideas, questions, an email is enough: [support@rosanero.app](mailto:support@rosanero.app). We usually reply within [n] working days.

<h2 id="privacy-dati">Privacy and data</h2>

What we collect, why and for how long, and your rights, are described in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy/). The terms of use of the app are in the [Terms and conditions](/en/terms/).

<h2 id="eliminazione">Deleting your account</h2>

You can delete your account directly from the app, in a few steps, or ask us by email. The full instructions are on the [Account deletion](/en/delete-account/) page.

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

**Is Rosanero the official Palermo app?**
No. It is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Palermo F.C. It does not use official logos or content.

**When is it coming out?**
The first features are being built. We announce them here and on the stores when they are ready, without dates we cannot keep.

**I deleted my account: can I come back?**
Yes, by registering again. But level, XP and collectibles cannot be recovered: deletion is final.
